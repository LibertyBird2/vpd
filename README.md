# V4D Voice

You are a Senior Product Designer, UX Architect, Accessibility Specialist, Brand Designer, and NGO Digital Experience Expert.

Design a complete high-fidelity interactive prototype for the official website of:

V4D — Voice for Persons with Disabilities

Do not generate backend logic or production code.

Focus on creating a polished, realistic prototype that demonstrates the visual identity, layout, user experience, accessibility, and information architecture.

Organization

V4D is a newly established rights-based organization in Yemen.

Important context:

The organization is in its foundation stage.

No official V4D projects have been launched yet.

V4D originated from two community initiatives.

One initiative implemented specialized digital awareness campaigns.

The other implemented community-based field projects.

These initiatives represent the roots of V4D but are not organizational projects.

The founder is a person with a disability.

The organization is rights-based rather than charity-driven.

Organization Purpose

Build a modern organization that works to advance the rights, dignity, participation, accessibility, inclusion, policy development, capacity building, research, and community engagement of persons with disabilities in Yemen.

Advocacy is one important tool, but it is not the organization's sole identity.
